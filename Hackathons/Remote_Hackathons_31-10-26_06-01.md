## Notes / Verification Caveats

- **Cutoff:** Events with a required registration deadline before 5 Nov 2026, 06:01 IST are excluded even if the Devpost submission deadline is later. For example, CodeTitans closes 4 Nov 20:30 IST; KUK Virtual closes 31 Oct KST; HackWithAditya requires an on-campus finale. Some newly found events specify a submission deadline but no separate registration close; they are included only where the organizer's registration/join path appears open.
- **No in-person final:** The public Devpost **HackWithAditya** listing is misleadingly labeled online; its organizer explicitly requires an on-campus round. HACKS 2026, DevHack 2026, STARFORGE, Echelon, DeveloperWeek 2027 (main-stage final), VOYAGEHACK 4.0 (remote final unconfirmed) and LPC Hacks are excluded or withheld. This is stricter than trusting the platform's online badge.
- **Previous reports:** SourceHacks V1, Cosmo Hacks 2026, PayPal AI Hackathon, HackTitan, Codegeist, UGREEN, CodeZen, Orbix, Arthaton, STEMergent, Meta VR Start, and other previously reported events were excluded. The 2027 **Build Beyond Hackathon** by **OSC** is distinct from similarly titled historical events; the organizer and start date differ.
- **SpectoV prize discrepancy:** The page advertises ₹5.5 lakh cash, but the itemized ₹2,00,000 + ₹1,00,000 + ₹50,000 + (30 × ₹10,000) adds up to ₹6.5 lakh. The lower advertised headline is not reconciled; confirm the actual payable pool before paying ₹149. Registration can close early at 250 seats.
- **CodePuneThinkers anomaly:** Unstop states registration closes 8 Nov 18:30 IST even though hacking begins 7 Nov 10:00 IST. The platform also shows $2,000 rank widgets that contradict the ₹30,000 total in the event description. The ₹30,000 description is used conservatively; register before the event begins if possible.
- **CodeNova terms:** Round 1/2 registration is free, but shortlisted round-3 teams must pay ₹399 per team. Unstop's rank widgets mention $2,000 while its description says ₹30,000 across the top three. Treat the USD figures as unverified, and internship/PPO claims as conditional.
- **AI BuildFest:** Adaption cash and credit awards are subject to final sponsor confirmation; using the required Adaption platform to train a model is mandatory. Cash is not the same as credits.
- **FUTUREHACK:** Advertised as a 36-hour hackathon, but the published build schedule from 15 Dec 12:30 to 17 Dec 12:30 spans 48 hours. The 17 Dec 12:30 IST submission cutoff is explicit. Webcam and screen/tab monitoring are mandatory.
- **Kiroween:** The official Devpost exclusions are broader than common default lists (including Australia, Indonesia, Italy and the Philippines). The 2026 rule requires **new projects started during 30 Oct–4 Dec**, not old projects with cosmetic changes.
- **Mindblown Cup:** Prize amounts are published by the jam host but independently verified payout conditions were not available. Mindblown AI is required. **Mascky Jam** is non-cash and disallows AI-generated game content.
- **Other withheld candidates:** HackTheFashion Global'26 has unresolved template placeholders such as [YOUR TRY-ON API NAME] and [API SIGN-UP URL], making its advertised $45,000 cash difficult to validate; Mathematics for systems security conflicts between a Nov 1 rulebook cutoff and Nov 15 Devpost header; CRC Hacks and ITESO SoftwareChido are invitation/course-restricted; DevIgnite returns HTTP 410; HACKRISE, Arcticathon and HackOut 2027 did not expose enough accessible official detail; 305 CodeFest had incomplete edition-specific submission rules. Not included in the qualifying table.
- **Deadline precision:** A date without a time is deliberately labeled as such; do not assume 11:59 PM. Platform/organizer schedules can change. Check the linked event immediately before applying.
