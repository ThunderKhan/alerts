The generic name **Global AI Hackathon** is distinguished from the previously recorded **Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon** by organizer and event dates.
- **Game jams included deliberately:** The scan's historical scope includes build-and-submit game jams; the table includes genuine remote development competitions and community jams, but excludes pure portfolio displays, generic coding quizzes, non-build giveaways and game jams with no meaningful submission process.
