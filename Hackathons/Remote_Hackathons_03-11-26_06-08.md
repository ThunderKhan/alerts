## Notes / Verification Caveats

- **Five-full-day filter:** At 3 Nov 2026, 06:08 IST, the earliest acceptable deadline is after 8 Nov 2026, 06:08 IST. ThinkyCon and Autumn Lisp close 9 Nov 05:00 UTC (10:30 IST), so both qualify. Earlier events, even if still displayed as “online,” were excluded.
- **No forced in-person organizer final:** QNX, AWS MSP, CIVIC, Veritas, Enginow and Hack4Hack are judged/presented remotely; the game jams submit digitally. Global Virtual Hackathon has a **mandatory team co-location requirement for its recorded interview**, although its judges/finals are online and exceptions may be requested. It does not require travel to an organizer venue; distributed teams should confirm an exception before registering.
- **Restricted participation:** AWS MSP AI is for people representing registered AWS Partner Network organizations, not the general public. CIVIC Hack requires at least one University of Waterloo student on each team. Veritas and Global Virtual are high-school-only. NovemBird is 18+. QNX requires 18+ and excludes some countries/territories. Do not treat the “Online/Public” Devpost label as proof of universal eligibility.
- **Enginow deadline conflict:** Enginow’s current organizer page says **registration open until 14 Nov 2026**, but an earlier Unstop listing titled “Built With Talent S1” closed in October. The new listing may be a reopened or migrated application route. The organizer's active registration page is cited, with the conflict clearly flagged; verify the portal accepts registrations before investing effort.
- **Prize accounting:** Veritas’s US$75,000+ is mostly scholarships and an internship, **not cash**. Hack4Hack and Enginow offer conditional interview opportunities, **not guaranteed jobs**. Game jams with no cash are explicitly marked as noncompetitive or community-focused.
- **Generative-AI policy:** Autumn Lisp, ThinkyCon, PJP, PROCJAM, LSDJAM, Bitsyjam and NovemBird explicitly ban generative AI. 20 Second Game Jam requires disclosure and bans autonomous AI-agent entrants. These restrictions apply even when AI coding assistants are otherwise commonly used.
- **Previously reported events excluded:** Innovate Online, Catalyst Solo AI, CodeNova India, Pixel Forge MEGA Jam, Bevy Jam #8, 20 Games Challenge Autumn, LÖVE Jam 2026, Official Unity Jam, Epic AI MegaJam, Godot Wild Jam #99, ShroomJam, Pizza Jam, 3x3x3 Jam, The MFG Gambler's Jam, and previously consolidated master entries. Mathematics for Systems Security, CRC Hacks and DeveloperWeek events had previously been excluded or flagged in earlier reports for eligibility/remote-final or rule uncertainties, so they were not recycled as new discoveries.
- **In-person finale exclusions:** NovaStark Hacks (Mumbai grand finale), AgentVerse national finale, HackWithAditya and similar hybrid-only finals were excluded. Short-cutoff and closed-registration events were excluded even when a later submission stage existed.
- **Quality threshold:** Each row is supported by a live organizer site or original organizer-hosted Devpost/itch.io page. Prize valuations, deadlines and eligibility can change; the original event page controls.
