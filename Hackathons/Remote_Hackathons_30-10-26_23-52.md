## Notes / Verification Caveats

- **Strict cutoff applied:** A deadline must be after 4 November 2026, 23:52 IST. The published deadline of the online **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon** is **4 November**, and its application deadline was **25 October**; it was excluded. Events whose cutoff is today or only a few days away (including No Hands: The Wispr Challenge and various short buildathons) were also excluded.
- **Misleading online listings excluded:** **LPC Hacks** is labeled Online on Devpost but its rules require in-person participation in Livermore, California. **Agentic Hackathon (Devin/Bangladesh)** is labeled Online but demands an in-person Dhaka final. Both are excluded.
- **Deadline before build date / closed registration:** **Scope-AI-Thon 2026** lists team sign-up for 30 October even though presentations are in November. It was excluded. **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon** had already closed applications and its final submission date does not satisfy five full days.
- **Ctrl+V discrepancy:** Its rules list 10 Nov 23:59 UTC, but its live Devpost schedule lists 10 Nov 5:00 PM EST; the earlier platform schedule is used. Devpost eligibility banner is students/legal majority, though body/rules say broader participants.
- **CSC Winter Sparkathon discrepancy:** Devpost's live schedule shows 30 Nov 12:00 AM PST while detailed rules say 12:00 PM PST. Participants should plan for the earlier midnight cutoff.
- **Out of Bounds prizes discrepancy:** Current Unstop text shows ₹3,000/₹1,000/₹500, while other snippets/mirrors show ₹10,000/₹1,000/₹500. The smaller detailed payout is used. A ₹19 participation fee applies.
- **CodeZen prize display discrepancy:** The official Unstop description quotes ₹30,000 across the top three, but ranking widgets also show $2,000 text; those USD numbers are not treated as confirmed additional cash. Sponsor credits and internships/PPOs are conditional.
- **Dakar Bitcoin** is geographically restricted to African residents, despite the broad Devpost geography badge. Prizes are cryptocurrency (sats), not fixed USD cash.
- **BASIS** currently allows future registration only from 22 November and is restricted to students enrolled in BASIS schools. It is included because its hackathon is upcoming and the registration deadline is far in the future.
- **Other exclusions:** Already recorded events such as **Homingo Hack Days**, **Meta VR Start 2026**, **Learn AR with SPECS**, **EthnoHack**, **Bridge The Gap Hacks**, **HackTitan**, **Arbiter Hacks V1**, **GDevelop BIG Game Jam #10**, **HackOdisha**, and the dozens of events in historical reports. **CodeClash November Nexus** and **TechFusion MERN Stack Challenge** are primarily MCQ/coding assessments, not build-and-submit hackathons. **Redshifted Cortex** advertises both an Ottawa onsite event and an online listing without sufficient independent remote-final detail, so it was excluded.
- **No completeness guarantee:** Some sources were inaccessible, stale or mirroring older events. Discoveries were retained only when supported by a current organizer site, official hosting platform or sufficiently detailed original organizer listing.
