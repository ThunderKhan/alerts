## Notes / Verification Caveats

- **Five-full-day threshold:** A deadline at or before 7 November 2026, 06:00 IST is excluded. Every included registration cutoff or submission deadline is later than that threshold. A later submission deadline does **not** prove late registration is permitted: read each event's rules before joining.
- **Already-reported events excluded:** The consolidated archive and the 31 October/1 November reports include Codegeist, UGREEN, Epic AI MegaJam, Official Unity Game Jam, thatgamejam #02, Innovate Online Hackathon, ML Gradient Wars, Pixel Forge MEGA Jam, Uplifting Game Jam #10, Micro Jam 070, and many others. Their different titles or aggregator aliases were not treated as new events.
- **Misleading online badges excluded:** K! Hacks 4.0, Srujan 2026, Quasar 5.0, STARFORGE, HACKDAY 2.0, Codesprint 2026, and similar contests require an **offline final**. DeveloperWeek 2027 and DeveloperWeek New York 2027 advertise an online build but list on-site final/award stages without a clearly independent remote-final entitlement; they were withheld. Operation Ward Zero displays a later registration date despite a **24 October submission cutoff** and 31 October results; excluded.
- **Conflicting dates excluded:** Mathematics for systems security has a **1 November 2026** project deadline in its rules even though its Devpost header shows **15 November**. The earlier rulebook deadline has already passed; it was excluded. HACKRISE'26 and HackTheFashion have incomplete or placeholder rules and were withheld.
- **Remote finals:** Spooky Jam explicitly permits participants to compete online, and games are judged via a streamed/downloaded presentation; **most physical awards require on-site presence**. No travel is required to submit and have the game considered. Other listed jams use online submission and remote judging/rating.
- **Prize accuracy:** Zoud's $140k is a combined maximum including conditional development funding. Pixel Forge's $30k+ headline is mainly in-kind and **already reported**. GenHacks awards are **CAD**, not USD. Micro Jam 069 advertises $300 in cash, but payouts depend on specific tool categories and eligibility. Sponsor credits, gift cards, software licenses, pizza and rankings are not cash.
- **Restricted eligibility/fees:** AgentRush USA and Model Mayhem USA require US residence; finalists pay $49/team and $30/team respectively, after free qualifiers. GenHacks is students age 13+; ML Empowerment requires students above the local legal age of majority; The MFG Gambler's Jam offers Hack Club physical prizes specifically to eligible teens.
- **AI policy varies substantially:** Agentware, ZIVOX, GenHacks and Ultimate AI-Powered Game Jam encourage or require AI; Godot Wild Jam, Mini Jam, Craftpix, ShroomJam, Hand Crafted Jam and several other game jams prohibit generated code, art or music. Consult each event's rules before reusing AI-generated material.
- **Future details not finalized:** Hack Era 2.0 does not publish the precise build window; Metroidvania Month Finale rules are scheduled for a 7 November reveal; GDKO 2027 prize wording contains a stale 2026 year. These uncertainties are disclosed rather than guessed.
- **Organizer page is authoritative:** The Lost PS2 Jam extended its deadline from an older 7 November date to **21 November**; the live itch.io jam page controls. Zoud's cutoff is **15 November, 23:59 Anywhere on Earth**, which can appear as 16 November on itch.io in other time zones.
